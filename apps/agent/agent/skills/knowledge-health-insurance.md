# Health insurance plans

## Coverage types

Individual and family health plans.

## Plan levels

- Bronze: lower premiums, higher out-of-pocket costs.
- Silver: balanced premiums and out-of-pocket costs. The most popular choice.
- Gold: higher premiums, lower out-of-pocket costs. Suits frequent care.
- Platinum: the most complete coverage, with the lowest out-of-pocket costs.

## Key features

- Preventive care: annual wellness exam, screenings and vaccinations at no cost.
- Prescription coverage with a tiered copay structure.
- A broad network of in-network providers.
- A choice of deductible levels.
- An annual out-of-pocket maximum.

## Special situations

- Pre-existing conditions: marketplace plans cannot exclude them. Do not discuss
  the lead's own conditions; the licensed agent covers that on the call.
- Maternity coverage is included on marketplace plans.
- Children can stay on a parent's plan until age 26.
- Federal tax credits can lower premiums. The licensed agent checks eligibility.

## Enrollment

- Open enrollment usually runs November to December for January coverage.
- A special enrollment period follows a major life event: marriage, a birth, or
  loss of other coverage.
- The start date depends on when the application is submitted.
