# The agency

## Who we are

An independent, licensed life and health insurance agency. A licensed agent
reviews each lead's situation on a short call and compares options.

## Licensed states

The task message lists them on a `Licensed states:` line. Use only that line.
When it says not set, do not name any state and do not promise coverage: a
licensed agent confirms on the call.

## What happens on the call

- About 20 minutes, by phone or video.
- A licensed agent asks about the lead's needs, explains the options that fit,
  and answers every question about rates, eligibility and coverage.
- There is no cost and no obligation to buy.
- The lead can bring a spouse or partner to the call.

## What the agent covers that you do not

Quotes, premiums, underwriting, health questions, eligibility, and comparisons
with a policy the lead already has.
