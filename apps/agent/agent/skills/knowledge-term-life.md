# Term life insurance

## Overview

Straightforward term life policies that replace income during the years a
family needs it most.

## Term options

- 10-year term: protection for an early career or a young family.
- 20-year term: coverage through the peak earning and family years.
- 30-year term: extended protection to near retirement.

## Coverage amounts

From $100,000 to $2,000,000, sized to the lead's income-replacement need. The
licensed agent sets the amount on the call.

## Key benefits

- Level premiums: the rate stays the same for the full term.
- Simple application: some applicants need no medical exam. The licensed agent
  confirms what applies on the call.
- Conversion: many policies can convert to permanent coverage later.
- No cash value: the premium pays for protection only, not investment features.

## Who it suits

- Protecting a mortgage or a large debt.
- Replacing income for dependents.
- Funding children's education.
- Business continuation for an owner.

## Common questions

- How fast can I get coverage? The licensed agent covers timing and eligibility
  on the call.
- What if my health changes later? The policy continues as issued for the term.
- Can I increase coverage? Term coverage is usually level. Conversion to
  permanent coverage can allow changes.

## Next step

A licensed agent walks through the lead's situation, confirms eligibility, and
answers rate and coverage questions on a quick call.
